# FujiSim Cam — roadmap

## Done
- v1: live recipe preview, photo capture, recipe editor
- v2: video (1080p30, sound, moving grain), log mode (10-bit HLG when available), iPhone-style Photographic Styles, grid + level, zoom, golden recipes
- v3: tap-to-focus box, on-screen exposure drag, AE/AF lock, 50MP toggle (Xiaomi limits apps to 12MP), iPhone-style styles screen
